## Passo 1: criar o Douglas (imagem)

No Flow, gere uma **imagem** (Nano Banana ou Imagen), formato **9:16**, **2 variações**. Escolha a melhor.

**Prompt A (só texto):**
```
3D animated movie character, Pixar / DreamWorks style caricature of a Brazilian man in his late 20s, selfie shot from slightly below, inside a car. Long oval face, very short black buzz-cut hair, thick straight dark eyebrows sitting low over heavy-lidded sleepy half-closed eyes, slightly furrowed brow, wide flat nose, full lips, one upper front tooth missing (visible gap when he talks), thin patchy stubble mustache, ears sticking out a little, warm medium-brown skin. Wearing a red sports t-shirt with a white stripe on the sleeves. Serious "I'm telling you the truth" expression, mouth slightly open mid-sentence. Background: car roof and side window, bright sunny sky and green trees outside, soft cinematic lighting, subsurface skin, highly detailed, vertical 9:16. No text, no captions, no watermark.
```

**Prompt B (se quiser subir um print do vídeo original como referência):**
```
Transform the man in this photo into a 3D animated movie character in Pixar / DreamWorks style. Keep his recognizable features: long oval face, buzz-cut hair, thick straight low eyebrows, heavy-lidded sleepy eyes, wide nose, full lips, missing upper front tooth, thin stubble mustache, red sports t-shirt with white stripe. Same selfie angle from below inside a car, sunny sky and trees in the window. Cartoon caricature, exaggerated but recognizable, vertical 9:16. No text, no watermark.
```
> O Flow pode recusar foto de pessoa real. Se recusar, use o Prompt A.

## Passo 2: folha de personagem (para manter o rosto igual em tudo)

Use a imagem escolhida como **referência** e gere:
```
Character turnaround sheet of this exact same 3D cartoon character: front view, three-quarter view and side profile, neutral standing pose from the waist up, same face, same buzz-cut, same eyebrows, same missing front tooth, same red sports t-shirt with white sleeve stripe. Plain light grey background, even studio lighting. No text.
```
Salve a imagem do Passo 1 e a folha como **ingredientes / personagem "Douglas"**.

## Passo 3: clipe de teste (vídeo, 8 s)

- Modelo: **Veo Fast** (gasta bem menos crédito que o Quality)
- **1 saída** por prompt
- 9:16
- **Frames to Video** com a imagem do Passo 1 como primeiro quadro (ou Ingredientes com o "Douglas")

**Prompt (fala dos 0 aos 6 s do áudio original):**
```
Handheld vertical selfie video inside a moving car, the same 3D Pixar-style character from the reference image. Sunny day, green trees passing quickly in the window behind him, natural phone camera shake. He talks straight to the camera with total conviction, frowning, sleepy heavy-lidded eyes, head bobbing with each word, pointing his finger at the camera on "você". He says in Brazilian Portuguese, fast and confident, a bit annoyed:
"Eu sou o Douglas, você não é o Douglas! Eu sou o Douglas, você não é o Douglas!"
Expressive lip-sync, missing front tooth visible when he talks. Car engine and road ambience. No subtitles, no on-screen text, no music.
```

### Depois do teste, me mande:

Com isso eu:

---

## Dicas para economizar crédito
- Sempre **Veo Fast** e **1 saída**. Quality só no clipe final, se precisar.
- Nada de texto ou efeito no prompt: o Flow erra letras. Os efeitos eu coloco depois, de graça.
- Se um clipe sair bom em 80%, **não refaça**. Eu corrijo na edição.
